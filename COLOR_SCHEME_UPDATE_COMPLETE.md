## Key Changes Made

1. **Tailwind Config**: Replaced Y2K neon colors with the new professional palette
2. **CSS Variables**: Updated root variables for consistent theming
3. **Component Styles**: Updated all brutalist cursor, hover, and animation colors
4. **Background**: Changed from white to sky blue (`#EAF4FF`)
5. **Text Colors**: Updated to deep navy (`#0F2E5A`) for better readability
6. **Accent Colors**: Replaced cyan with teal accent (`#2EC4B6`)
7. **Interactive Elements**: Updated hover and focus states with new colors

The update maintains the brutalist aesthetic while providing a more professional and cohesive color scheme.
